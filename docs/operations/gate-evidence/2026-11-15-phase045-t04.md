## What landed (T-04)

- **The attendance store** (`modules/hr/infrastructure/attendance.store.ts`):
  capture, the one-shot checkout fill (`WHERE check_out IS NULL` — the
  law's floor), the derivation write (status fields ONLY), the
  synthesized absence row, the derivation-window loads (roster cells
  with the shift instants computed in the TENANT's timezone — a
  declared night crossing ends on work_date + 1; the tenant-jurisdiction
  holidays; the APPROVED leave spans), the register + exception reads,
  and the punch-envelope sanity check. All time arithmetic rides
  integer milliseconds extracted in SQL (`EXTRACT(EPOCH …) * 1000`) —
  never float-parsed date strings; the minute facts are integer floor
  divisions via the house bitwise pattern (ADR-0009 bans `Math.floor`).
- **The attendance service** (`modules/hr/application/attendance-service.ts`):
  - **capture** — a session OPENS with a check-in (the 0081
    session-shape law); INV-031's non-overlap (an OPEN session extends
    to +∞ and blocks every later session until closed) and the 0081
    dedupe floor (the same employee + timestamp is ONE session) are
    typed refusals BEFORE any write; the punch envelope (both
    timestamps on the work date or the day after, the tenant's
    timezone) and the device/geo shapes are typed too;
  - **checkout** — BR-082's only punch-mutating arm: check_out fills
    EXACTLY ONCE; a second fill, a checkout of an already-closed
    session, or a checkout of a derived absence marker (it carries no
    session) are all the typed `ATTENDANCE_IMMUTABLE` refusal —
    corrections arrive as NEW rows;
  - **the derivation** — THE DAY IS THE UNIT, never the row:
    `HOLIDAY` (the tenant-jurisdiction calendar) > `ON_LEAVE` (an
    APPROVED span — a holiday is never charged to a leave balance) >
    the operational family (`ABSENT` / `MISSING_PUNCH` / `LATE` /
    `PRESENT`), with the exact minute facts: late past grace, early
    leave, worked minus the shift's breaks, overtime past the shift
    end; an unrostered day with punches derives `PRESENT` with the raw
    span and NO shift-relative facts (the BR-055 no-fabricated-defaults
    discipline); every write derives the day immediately — a row never
    rests with a stale verdict;
  - **the ABSENT synthesis** — a rostered day that already happened,
    with no punch, no holiday, no leave, becomes the append-only
    `(null, null)` marker row (source MANUAL is the only shape the
    0081 CHECK family allows a synthesized row; the note names it
    `DERIVED_ABSENT`); NEVER for a future day (the tenant-tz today
    guard) or an unrostered one; a later real punch re-derives the day
    and the marker follows the verdict (append-only, never deleted);
  - **the exception set** — `ABSENT`, `MISSING_PUNCH`, and `LATE`
    (past grace) carry `is_exception = true`; early leave and overtime
    stay FACTS (no invented thresholds — a threshold would be a
    policy, and none exists);
  - **the reads** — the daily register (joined with the employee +
    shift dims) and the paginated exception board (`total` + `hasMore`
    — the joint-review pagination law);
  - **the derive run** — a bounded backfill (≤ 92 days, the rotation
    precedent), the employeeIds case law (c537113: normalized to
    canonical lowercase before the uniqueness comparison, typed
    refusal on a case-varied duplicate), and IDEMPOTENT by
    construction: an unchanged verdict writes NOTHING (no version
    churn), the derivation write touches status fields only.
- **The routes** (`api/v1/hr/attendance/…`, presentation/
  `attendance.controller.ts`): `POST records` (the capture body schema
  exported for the module's unit surface — the rotationBody
  precedent), `POST records/:id/checkout`, `GET register?date=`,
  `GET exceptions?from=&to=` (paginated), `POST derive` — every row
  `@RequirePermissions("hr.attendance")` and registered in
  ROUTE_PERMISSIONS (the §8 registry law — no guarded route without a
  row).
- **The audit trail:** `hr.attendance_captured` / `hr.attendance_checkout`
  / `hr.attendance_derived` (the derivation event carries the counts).
- **THE DATED T-03 FILTER REPAIR (2026-11-15):** the T-03 hr error
  family landed without its error-filter rows, so `ShiftNotFoundError`
  and the hr `EmployeeNotFoundError` fell through to the 422 default —

  shared-gates sweep (the same shape as the 59085a0 round's
  permissions-drift spec repair); the codes never changed, the
