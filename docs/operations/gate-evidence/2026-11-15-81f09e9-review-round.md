### 3. THE §4 QUEUE-HEALTH DRAIN WINDOW (`e1d8712` — performance)

test failed — the pg-boss non-system load-depth sample stepped 5 → 7

required B ≤ A + 1: in the full-suite serial context (one process,
one shared DB — every earlier spec's residue draining behind the GA

adjacent-pair shape conflated the two.

**The fix keeps the signal honest and gives it a window:** sample up
to 12 times, 1 s apart, and require the depth to COME BACK DOWN to
the window's opening level (+1) within it. A real runaway (the
consumer dead, jobs piling faster than they drain) never comes back
down and still fails after the window; a transient burst drains and
passes. The absolute bound (≤ 50), the dead-letter budget
(failed delta = 0), and the total-depth census bound (baseline + 40)
are all unchanged; the whole drain trace prints for the evidence log.
