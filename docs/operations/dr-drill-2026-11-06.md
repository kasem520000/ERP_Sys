## 1. Scenario + declaration

**Scenario (S1, DISASTER_RECOVERY §1):** "Cloud region unavailable (network/power/provider
incident)" — recovery path "Cross-region restore (BACKUP_RESTORE_RUNBOOK §7)", targets
**RTO ≤ 8 h / RPO ≤ 15 min (backup replication lag)**.

**Detection (INCIDENT_RESPONSE §3.1, MONITORING §3).** First signals: LB health-check
failures across `web`/`api` nodes, `Api5xxSpike` (5xx > 2 % for 5 min → Sev-2 page;
> 10 % → Sev-1), `WorkerHeartbeatStale` (> 5 min), plus the provider's status incident.
Cold-walk finding F1 (§4): the written runbooks do not state **where the alerting stack
runs relative to the app region** — if monitoring is in-region it dies with the region
and detection falls to provider status + tenant reports; nothing in MONITORING §3/§4 or
OBSERVABILITY settles it cold.

**Triage + severity (INCIDENT_RESPONSE §1, §3.2).** All tenants down, region-wide,
provider ETA unknown → **SEV-1 by scope**, applying §3.2's "when in doubt, round severity
**up**". SLAs engaged (§1): ack ≤ 15 min (on-call), all-hands within 1 h, continuous work
until contained, customer comms ≤ 4 h. Cold-walk finding F2: the §1 ladder is
security/integrity-worded ("Platform-wide security or integrity event") — a pure
availability loss is not enumerated; the on-call reaches SEV-1 only via the round-up rule.
Recorded as a gap (§4) with a doc-fix action on the ladder's examples.

**Declaration.** The **incident commander declares** — per INCIDENT_RESPONSE §2, "IC =
On-call engineer (rotating): runs the incident, declares severity, owns timeline". On
declaration: incident channel + timeline doc opened (§3.2), internal first message per
§5's template (🚨 SEV-1 declared …, IC named, next update in 30 min), and the
**status-page banner goes up within 15 min** (DISASTER_RECOVERY §3: "Incident banner
within 15 min of Sev-1 declaration; updates every 30 min").

## 3. Decision points

**(a) Read-only mode (DISASTER_RECOVERY §2).** §2 scopes the switch to "S3/S5
deliberations … the incident commander may switch tenants to read-only … a platform flag
(ops-controlled), announced per §3, and its activation writes an audit event." For **S1
the walk does NOT switch it at declaration**: the region is down — there is nothing
running that could corrupt the ledger ("writes can't corrupt what can't run", §2.1's
reasoning). The decision arrives **post-restore**: if §7 step 4 verification (§3 step 4
invariant smoke + row-count parity) shows any anomaly, the IC holds the platform in
read-only/maintenance until clean — reads and reports available, posting refused — before
the DNS/LB cutover. Honest assessment of the mechanism: the **enforcement machinery is
proven in-platform at tenant scope** (the PHASE-014 read-only sweep — every
state-changing route returns the typed 403 `tenancy/subscription-read-only` while
reads/reports/exports continue), but the **ops-controlled platform-wide switch + its
activation audit event are not a distinct implemented surface today** (gap G5, §4).

**(b) Cutover vs wait — backup recency (§1 S1 RPO ≤ 15 min "backup replication lag").**
The walk's arithmetic: §7 step 3 restores "nightly dump restore (or snapshot+PITR where
the provider supports cross-region)". With **nightly-dump-only** replication the recovery
point is the last 02:00 dump — RPO up to ~24 h, not ≤ 15 min; **the ≤ 15 min S1 claim
presumes the WAL archive (or provider cross-region PITR) replicates cross-region within
15 min**. The runbook's §1 job table lists `wal-archive` as provider-managed but never
states that the WAL storage replicates cross-region. Decision recorded: restore the
**freshest replicated artifact immediately** (the RTO ≤ 8 h clock is running; waiting
cannot conjure a fresher artifact that does not exist), and quantify the data window at
all-clear time from the artifact's timestamp. Gap G1 (§4) makes the ≤ 15 min RPO claim
conditional until the provider checklist provisions cross-region WAL/PITR replication.

**(c) Tenant comms (§3 templates — the artifacts used, quoted verbatim).** Sent by the
communications lead (IC delegates; product owner when available — INCIDENT_RESPONSE §2),
within the SEV-1 ≤ 4 h customer-comms SLA (§1), status page carrying the detail:

- **Disruption notice** — AR: «نواجه اضطرابًا في الخدمة حاليًا، ونعمل على استعادتها. سيتم
  تحديث هذه الصفحة كل ٣٠ دقيقة.» / EN: "We are experiencing a service disruption and
  are working on recovery. This page is updated every 30 minutes."
- **Read-only mode** (only if decision (a) fires post-restore) — AR: «النظام في وضع
  القراءة فقط مؤقتًا لحماية بياناتكم أثناء المعالجة.» / EN: "The system is temporarily
  in read-only mode to protect your data during recovery."
- **All-clear + restoration statement** — AR: «تمت استعادة الخدمة. المعلومات المسجلة
  خلال الفترة (التوقيت المذكور) قد تحتاج لإعادة الإدخال.» / EN: "Service is restored.
  Entries recorded during the affected window (time noted) may need to be re-entered."

**(d) Status-page cadence (§3).** Banner **within 15 min** of SEV-1 declaration; updates
**every 30 min**; root-cause summary **within 72 h** of closure (§3 "After" column; the
public postmortem summary links within 10 business days per INCIDENT_RESPONSE §5). The
walk pre-drafted the banner text (the §3 disruption notice) at T+15 and set the 30-min
update discipline for the ops channel timeline (§3 "Live timeline, decisions,
timestamps").

**Tabletop clock (decision sequence, not wall-clock evidence).** T+0 detection → T+10 IC
ack + SEV-1 declaration (§1 ack SLA ≤ 15 min) → T+15 status-page banner + internal
notice → T+20 tenant notice drafted (AR+EN) → T+30 engineering lead joins (MONITORING §4:
after 30 min on an active Sev-1) + product owner joins for the data-loss/comms decisions
(§4) → T+45 §7 step 1 replication-currency check + restore-point decision (3b) →
provision recovery region (§7 step 2, hosted leg) → restore (§7 step 3) → verify §3
step 4 + row-count parity (§4 step 5) → read-only/maintenance hold only on anomaly (3a)
→ api → web → worker last → cutover DNS/LB (§7 step 4, RTO ≤ 8 h budget) → all-clear +
restoration statement (§3) → post-incident review within 5 business days (§6).
